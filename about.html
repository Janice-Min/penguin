A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Janice Jungbin.

I enjoy spending time on my own, thinking up little ideas and making things with my hands.

Hi! I'm Janice Jungbin Min, a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Sungkyunkwan University and an undergraduate researcher at BEAR Lab.

I'm interested in robotics, especially robot design, simulation, and the use of AI in robotics. I've had the chance to explore different areas, from evaluating foundation models for robot navigation at Carnegie Mellon University to designing tendon-driven robotic hands and experimenting with robot control. These days, I'm particularly interested in how robots can be designed for different tasks.

Outside of research, I love watching musicals, reading novels, and cross-stitching. I enjoy getting lost in a good story, whether it's on stage or in a book, and spending quiet afternoons working on a cross-stitch project.

This website is a little collection of things I've worked on, things I'm interested in, and a few things I simply like. 🐧
