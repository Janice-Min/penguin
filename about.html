A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Janice Jungbin.

I'm happiest when I get to connect a theoretical idea to a robot that actually moves.

I'm an undergraduate student in Mechanical Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University and an undergraduate researcher in the BEAR Lab, interested in the space where robot mechanisms, computation and intelligent behavior meet.

My experiences span evaluating foundation models for robot navigation as a visiting scholar at Carnegie Mellon University, tendon-driven hand design, a self-leveling tray, and controller comparisons in simulation. Across these projects, I keep coming back to one question: how can we design better physical robots by thinking about their task, intelligence and mechanics together?

Outside the polished results, I enjoy the messy, iterative process of building: writing simulation code, testing a mechanism, discovering what failed, and trying again.
