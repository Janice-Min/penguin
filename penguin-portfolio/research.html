INTERESTS / QUESTIONS / DIRECTION

What I'm curious about.

How do we build robots whose physical design and intelligence are considered together, rather than separately?

RESEARCH AREA / 01

AI-driven Robot Design

From a task description to feasible mechanical design choices, using language/vision models together with physical simulation and optimization.

RESEARCH AREA / 02

Underactuated & Tendon-driven Mechanisms

Compact robotic hands, graspable workspaces, passive adaptation, and thoughtful trade-offs in mechanical complexity.

RESEARCH AREA / 03

Control, Autonomy & Reliability

Understanding what makes robot behavior robust through systematic evaluation, feedback control and learning-based methods.

HOW I LIKE TO WORK
