HOW I LIKE TO WORK

A research workflow

Each step with an example from the tendon-driven gripper project.

STEP 01

Ask

Form a clear problem and a measurable objective.

e.g. Which finger geometry grasps the widest range of object sizes and positions?

STEP 02

Model

Translate the question into a physical or computational system.

e.g. Parametric two-finger tendon models in MuJoCo.

STEP 03

Experiment

Simulate, optimize, validate and examine failures.

e.g. Graspable workspace maps and a CMA-ES design search.

STEP 04

Iterate

Learn from results and improve the next design.

e.g. 3D-print the best design and test it on real objects.
